INDIE
STUDIO

SMALL TEAM.
BIG CAMPFIRES.

Lantern Works is a two-person indie studio nestled somewhere between a forest and a deadline. We make games the way you'd tell a campfire story — slowly, warmly, and with just the right amount of mystery.

Founded in 2021 by a wandering programmer and a pixel-obsessed artist, we believe games should feel like coming home after a long journey. No frenetic scoreboards, no grinding loops — just worlds worth getting lost in.

Every sprite is hand-drawn, every sound recorded in our gloriously drafty studio. We ship when things feel right, not when a publisher says so.
