About Lantern Works

We are a passionate indie game studio dedicated to creating warm, engaging games that bring joy and comfort to players worldwide.

Our mission is simple: craft games that feel like a warm campfire on a cozy evening—inviting, meaningful, and memorable. Every project we undertake is infused with care, creativity, and a genuine love for the craft.

From intimate storytelling to relaxing gameplay mechanics, Lantern Works believes games should be a sanctuary. Join us as we illuminate the indie game landscape with light and warmth.
